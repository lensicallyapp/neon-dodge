# Neon Dodge

A polished, dependency-free arcade survival browser game. Move left/right to dodge pink hazards and collect gold energy. The storm speeds up as you survive.

## Play

Open `dist/index.html` in any modern browser or serve `dist/` as a static site. Deployed on Cloudflare Pages.

- **Desktop:** Left/right arrows or A/D to steer; P or Space to pause.
- **Mobile:** Touch and drag inside the game area.
- **Audio:** Optional sound toggle (off by default).
- **Scoring:** Earn points by surviving and picking up gold energy. High score is saved locally in your browser.

## Technology

Plain HTML, CSS, and canvas JavaScript. No build tools, frameworks, or network dependencies. The contents of `dist/` are deployment-ready.
